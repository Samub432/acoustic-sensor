# holder: 3D-printed disc holder

Holds the two piezo discs face to face on opposite walls of a Sarstedt T25 culture flask at a fixed height.

| File | What it is |
|---|---|
| `Acoustic Sensor - Holder.f3d` | Fusion 360 model with its full design history: edit it to adapt the holder to another vessel. |
| `Acoustic Sensor - Holder.stl` | Ready to print. |

## How it works

- Two arms, each ending in a round cup that holds one Ø 27 mm disc. The wires leave through the opening at the back of the cup.
- Both arms slide along the base. Push each one in until its disc rests against the flask wall: friction holds it in place, no screws needed.
- Press gently. Pushing harder squeezes the couplant out of the gap and weakens the contact.

## Printing and checks

- PLA or PETG. PETG is the better choice if you clean the holder with disinfectants (never immerse PLA in bleach).
- Before applying any couplant, check that each disc sits flat in its cup and that both arms slide smoothly but stay where you leave them.
